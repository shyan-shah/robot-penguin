Meet the robot penguin I've been building.

23 cm tall. Seven servos. A speaker, a microphone, and a beak that actually moves when he talks.

He waddles. He starts dancing if he hears music. He learns — say something to him enough times and he starts remembering how to answer without asking the internet.

And he has a little "help me" waggle for when he falls over and can't get back up. Which, after a genuinely embarrassing amount of effort trying to fix it, turns out to be always.

Here's the thing though: everything you're looking at is a simulation.

He has walked thousands of times inside a physics engine and never once touched an actual floor.

That changes this week. The print files are finished and the first parts go on the printer tonight.

I'm more nervous about this than I expected to be. Simulations are very agreeable places. Friction is whatever you tell it to be, nothing ever warps, no screw is ever 2 mm too long, and the carpet doesn't have opinions.

Real life is about to have a lot of opinions.

Looking forward to finding out which bits I got wrong. Will post the first steps — successful or otherwise.
